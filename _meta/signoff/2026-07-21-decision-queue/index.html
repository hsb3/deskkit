2 · ADR 0020: keep the strict claim rule, or soften it back to advisory?

When one actor (a person or agent) holds a claim on a work item, the shipped behavior refuses every direct change to that item by anyone else — not just status transitions, but also block/unblock and field edits. This was accepted at pull-request merge under the delivery plan's "PR review is the ruling" mechanism; you reserved the right to overrule. The alternative reading gates only transitions and leaves field edits open, which re-creates the silent-overwrite problem the finding flagged. Stale claims expire after 30 minutes, and a manual release command exists.

Recommendation: confirm the strict rule. It is one uniform, already-tested behavior. If you prefer advisory, the ADR lists the exact three code checks to revert, byte-for-byte.

Standard

4 · Make the three brownfield-retrofit defaults official standard behavior?

Ruling these makes retrofit reproducible for any adopter instead of a one-off judgment call.

Recommendation: approve all three as ruled defaults (and record retrofit-over-exempt as a supported disposition). I amend the conventions standard and the brownfield skill accordingly.
