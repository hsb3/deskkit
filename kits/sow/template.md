# Statement of Work: {Client} — {Engagement name}

_The client-facing contract for an engagement: what we'll do, what we'll hand over, how we'll know it's done, and on what terms. This is the document the client signs — keep it precise, keep it honest, and never promise here what the internal plan can't deliver._

_Why this engagement exists, in the client's words. The situation they're in, what prompted the work, and the one or two outcomes they care about most. Two or three short paragraphs — enough that a reader who's never met the client understands the brief._
